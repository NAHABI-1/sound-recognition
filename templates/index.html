How Fourier Analysis Helps

Sound is a changing pressure wave. Fourier Transform breaks that wave into frequency components, making it easier to identify pitch, energy, speech patterns, and noisy regions.

The FFT graph reveals which frequencies are strongest, while the spectrogram shows how those frequencies change throughout the audio.
